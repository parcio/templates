\section{Guidelines}

The first chapters should use generally understandable terms, while later chapters can introduce and make use of technical terms.
Each chapter should contain a short introduction and a summary.

The number of pages depends on the type of thesis.
While a bachelor's thesis is usually in the 40--50 pages range, a master's thesis often has around 80--100 pages.

The colloquium consists of a 20 (bachelor) or 25 (master) minute presentation, followed by a question and answer session.

\section{Structure}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Abstract (1 page)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item A short version of the whole thesis
	\end{itemize}
	\item Introduction (5--10\,\%)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Motivates and introduces the overall thesis
		\begin{itemize}
			\item “This thesis makes the following major contributions…”
			\item “… is completely new because of …”
		\end{itemize}
		\item Outlines the structure
		\begin{itemize}
			\item “The remainder of the thesis is organized as follows...”
		\end{itemize}
	\end{itemize}
	\item Background/State of the Art (10--20\,\%)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item A collection of background information necessary to understand the rest of the thesis
	\end{itemize}
	\item Design and Implementation (25--35\,\%)
	\item Related Work (5--10\,\%)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Presents similar works and compares them to the approach taken in the thesis
	\end{itemize}
	\item Evaluation (25--35\,\%)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Detailed description of the test environment
		\item Interpretation of obtained results
	\end{itemize}
	\item Conclusion and Future Work (5--10\,\%)
\end{enumerate}
